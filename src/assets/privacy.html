Privacy Notice

Website enquiry information

When you submit a contact enquiry through this website, DTYedidyah Engineering receives the information you provide, such as your name, company, business email, phone number, service of interest and project message.

We use submitted details to respond to your enquiry, assess project requirements, communicate with you and maintain enquiry records for business follow-up.

Enquiry records are accessible to authorized personnel for business purposes. We retain information only as long as needed for legitimate business follow-up, legal obligations or recordkeeping, and then delete or anonymize it according to our retention practices.

Your requests

For questions or requests regarding information submitted through this website, contact sales@dtyedidyah.com.
